The Complete Reference of Finance

Batch 1 live · Introduction and Part I, Foundations of Money

One book for everything about money and investing, from what a rupee really is to derivatives, fintech and building a portfolio. It is written the way a forty-year market veteran would write it for a reader who wants to master the subject: every idea rebuilt from first principles, every section carrying worked examples with real numbers, and every section ending with questions that make you recollect and apply what you read.

New parts are published here as they are written. Read in order if you are starting out; jump straight to a chapter if you are not.

How this book teaches

Most finance books hand down rules: buy low, diversify, never sell options. This book goes underneath the rules. First principles means starting from what is provably true and rebuilding every idea from there, so you understand why something works before memorising what to do. Rules break when conditions change; principles travel.

  • First-principles explanation: every topic is rebuilt from five basic building blocks: money, time, risk, exchange and information.
  • Worked examples and cases: concrete Indian and global examples with real numbers, so nothing stays theoretical.
  • Active-recall questions: at the end of every section, so you test yourself instead of only reading.
