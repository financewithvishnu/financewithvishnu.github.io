Site analytics and privacy

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how many consenting visitors read its pages. Analytics is off until you choose Allow analytics. If you decline, all educational content remains available.

When allowed, Google Analytics uses cookies and processes page addresses, page titles, device/browser information, approximate location and visit activity. This helps measure visits, page views and which pages are read. It does not identify every individual reader, and the count will exclude people who decline or block analytics.

We do not ask Analytics to collect your name, email, phone number or account details. Advertising personalization and Google Signals are not enabled. We remove query strings and fragments from page addresses sent by our tag. We do not use analytics to give investment advice.

You can change your choice at any time using Analytics preferences at the bottom of any page. Choosing Decline stops measurement and clears this site's Analytics cookies in that browser. Your choice is stored in that browser.

Google also processes data under its own privacy policy. Read Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
