Your first route

Safety and process before stock selection. This route follows the sequence explained in Chapter 85.

  1. Money and your finances: read Chapters 1-7 in order. Begin with Chapter 1.
  2. Funds, ETFs and fixed income: read Chapters 38-45. Continue with Chapter 38.
  3. Your portfolio and behaviour: read Chapters 75-79. Continue with Chapter 75.
  4. Then study companies and stocks: read Chapters 8-11, followed by 12-37. Start company analysis.
