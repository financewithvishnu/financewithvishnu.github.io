83.6How to use this sheet

Three habits make the sheet earn its place. Check the assumptions before the arithmetic: CAGR assumes one flow, YTM assumes reinvestment at the yield, Sharpe assumes roughly symmetric returns, and the chapters listed beside each table carry the failure modes. Keep units consistent, monthly rates with monthly counts, real with real, most formula errors are unit errors wearing arithmetic's clothes. And sanity-check every output against an anchor you already own: doubling times, the 4% rule's 25x, the Chapter 78 retirement arithmetic. A formula is a lever; anchors are the ground it pushes against.
